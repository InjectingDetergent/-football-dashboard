Model and assumptions

Each division is fitted separately to this season’s completed league matches, with opponent attack and defence strengths and home advantage. More recent matches receive greater weight, with a 90-day half-life. Team strengths are pulled towards the division average to reduce the effect of small samples.

Remaining matches are simulated with independent Poisson goal counts and fixed fitted strengths. Missing xG uses actual goals. Current points adjustments are retained. Positions use points, goal difference and goals scored; any remaining ties are split randomly. Injuries, transfers, future deductions and later tie-break criteria are outside this first model.

The points ranges reflect simulated match outcomes with fixed strengths. They do not include uncertainty in the estimated strengths. Model choices use one chronological holdout and have not been validated across multiple seasons. Regular-season finishing positions are shown; play-off outcomes are outside this simulation.
